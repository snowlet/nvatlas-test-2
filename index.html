About

What is NV Atlas?

NV Atlas (“Nominals to Verbs”) is a curated collection of real English sentences paired with natural Chinese translations, where the target nominal has been identified and its verb form supplied. It is designed to help language learners, translators, and AI agents see how English expresses actions through nouns — and how Chinese naturally re-expresses them as verbs.

🎯

Nominal → Verb

Each row flags the English nominal (e.g. question) and gives the verb that carries the meaning in Chinese (e.g. 问).
